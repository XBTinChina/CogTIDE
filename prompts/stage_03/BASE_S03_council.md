# Stage 3 — Council Synthesis (base)

Stage 3 is **not** another round of compression. It is **council
synthesis**: a small council made of 3 already-deep theories (from
Stage 2) plus 5–7 of the experts behind them is convened, and the
council tries to find a deeper architecture that those 3 deep theories
share.

## What "deeper" means at this stage

A kernel is deeper than the 3 input deep theories when:

- it identifies a **shared substrate** — a structural commitment, a
  computational primitive, an architectural constraint — that the 3
  theories all rest on, possibly without realizing it;
- the substrate is **specific** enough to be defended or refuted, not
  a vague handwave;
- it **explains why each of the 3 theories is locally adaptive** in
  its own slice of the question, while still being projections of the
  same deeper thing;
- it **preserves** rather than dissolves the productive tensions
  between the 3 theories. A kernel that erases tensions to look tidy
  is a worse kernel than one that names them and contains them.

A kernel is **not**:

- a literal average of the 3 input deep theories;
- a renaming exercise;
- a higher-level abstraction so generic it could absorb anything;
- a paraphrase of one of the 3 theories with the others mentioned in
  passing.

## Roleplay discipline

When the facilitator simulates the council, the 3 deep theories speak
through the experts who originated them. Each expert must remain
specific to their tools and vocabulary. Disagreements are valuable
data; do not whitewash them.

## Preservation marks

If any input deep theory carries `UNIQUE`, `RISKY`, or `SPECIAL`
material, the kernel must remain visible to that material — either by
naming it explicitly or by leaving structural room for it.

## Framework aids

The shared **Framework aids** prompt (above) offers classical lenses
from the philosophy of science — Marr's three levels, Tinbergen's
four questions, descriptive/mechanistic/normative,
cybernetic/control-theoretic, dynamical systems, plus Popper
falsifiability, Occam parsimony, and explanatory-vs-predictive
power. They can help sharpen what kind of substrate the kernel is
committing to (a computational-level substrate? a mechanism? a
normative commitment?). Use them when they help; leave them out
when they do not — the "aids, not bins" discipline overrides.
