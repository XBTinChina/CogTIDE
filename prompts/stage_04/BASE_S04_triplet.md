# Stage 4 — Triplet Elaboration (base)

Stage 4 is **not** broad synthesis and **not** cross-kernel comparison.
It is **local triplet elaboration**: for each Stage 3 kernel, a small
local panel of 5–7 experts produces a triplet of theories that
together form a usable theory family for that kernel:

- the **core theory** — the strongest faithful expression of the
  parent kernel;
- the **solid theory** — a more conservative, more defensible,
  narrower version;
- the **risky theory** — a bolder, more speculative, more generative
  version that still remains coherent.

Each kernel is handled strictly on its own terms. Do not compare to
other kernels. Do not borrow material from other kernels. The panel
sees only the parent kernel and (optionally) the experts' own
contextual knowledge of their own ideas.

## Triplet discipline

- **The three theories must be visibly different.** They are not
  paraphrases of each other. The differences should be in
  commitments, scope, and risk — not just wording.
- **The core theory must remain faithful** to the parent kernel; it
  should not drift into a different substrate.
- **The solid theory must not become trivial.** A safer version is
  still a theory, not a tautology.
- **The risky theory must remain coherent.** Bold is not the same as
  incoherent. The risky theory is bold *and* internally consistent.
- **Preservation respect.** If the parent kernel carries `UNIQUE`,
  `RISKY`, or `SPECIAL` material, the triplet (especially the risky
  theory) should keep that material visible.

## Framework aids

The shared **Framework aids** prompt (above) offers classical lenses
— Marr's three levels, Tinbergen's four questions,
descriptive/mechanistic/normative, cybernetic, dynamical systems,
plus Popper falsifiability, Occam parsimony, and explanatory-vs-
predictive power. They are particularly useful at Stage 4 for
sharpening the **difference** between the core, solid, and risky
theories: for example, the solid variant may pull the claim toward
a narrower computational level, while the risky variant may push
it into cross-level or more speculative predictive territory. Use
the lenses when they sharpen the triplet's distinctions; ignore
them when they do not — "aids, not bins" overrides.
