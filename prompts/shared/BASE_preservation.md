# Preservation discipline

Some ideas carry preservation marks: `UNIQUE`, `RISKY`, or `SPECIAL`. These
marks signal that the idea is structurally distinctive, takes a defensible
risk, or contains a perspective that should not be quietly absorbed into
mainstream synthesis. When merging, distilling, or summarizing, you must
keep marked ideas visible — never bury them in a generic category or drop
them in favor of more conventional alternatives.
